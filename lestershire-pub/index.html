About the pub

Great food and drinks in a cozy, clean, fun and friendly atmosphere. Come by for dine-in or take-out, sit outside when the weather’s good, and ask about seasonal leagues.

We’re a neighborhood pub at 85 CFJ Blvd in Johnson City, known around town for wings — especially Wednesday Wing Night. Follow along on Facebook for the latest specials.
