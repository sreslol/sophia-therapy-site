Welcome in

Pints, pizza & a stage on Front Street

Tucked into historic downtown Owego, Jon Barleycorn Tavern & Lounge is a small, friendly tavern with a big love for local music. Pull up a stool for local craft beer, wine and cocktails, catch a band, and stick around for a slice of our homemade pizza.

Out back, the porch looks right over the Susquehanna River — the best seat in town on a summer night. Inside, it’s live bands, comedy, trivia and good company.
