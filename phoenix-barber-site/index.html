Est. 2024 · Formerly Studio 11

Classic barbering, reborn

The Phoenix Barber Lounge, previously known as Studio 11, keeps the classics of barbering alive while meshing them with the modern day. Every cut starts with a consultation and finishes with the details: hot lather, hot towel, clean lines.

Step past the red curtains and settle into black leather by the fireplace. Out on the floor, ring-light mirrors line the stations over warm wood floors.

The shop’s roots are in community, and everyone is welcome in the chair: a parent bringing the kids, a college student looking for their barber for the next four years, or a dad trying a new style. The goal is simple. Feel right at home.

  • Walk-ins welcome, appointments recommended
  • Kid friendly, with a snack bar on site
  • Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex, debit and cash
